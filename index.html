Street Football Legends

Fast 1v1 arcade street football for iPhone and iPad. Rise from the neighborhood court to legend status, one rival at a time.

Need help?

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Email us at aduskun@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

  • Move: the left and right buttons (or A/D, ←/→ on a keyboard).
  • Jump: the up button (or W, ↑, Space). Tap again in the air to double jump.
  • Kick: the boot button (or X/J). Kick in the air for volleys, headers and bicycle kicks.
  • Special shot: when your special bar is full, tap the flame button (or Z/Shift) and your next kick becomes your hero's signature shot.

Common questions

How do I unlock new levels? Beat the rival in a level to unlock the next one.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Does the game need an internet connection? No, it plays fully offline.
