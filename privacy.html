Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how the Street Football Legends app ("the app") handles your information.

We do not collect personal data

The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no user accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The career mode works fully offline.

Online matches with Game Center

The optional 1v1 online mode uses Apple Game Center. If you choose to play online, Apple's Game Center service handles sign-in, friend invites and matchmaking, and your Game Center display name is shown to your opponent. During a match, only gameplay data (for example player positions, ball position, button presses and the score) is sent between the two players' devices through Game Center. This data is used only to run the match and is not stored by us. We do not receive or keep any of your Game Center information. Apple's handling of Game Center data is described in Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Data stored on your device

To save your progress, the app stores game data such as unlocked levels, stars, coins, upgrades, your selected character and your sound setting locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
