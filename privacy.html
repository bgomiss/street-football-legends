Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Street Football Legends app ("the app") handles your information.

We do not collect personal data

The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

To save your progress, the app stores game data such as unlocked levels, stars, coins, upgrades, your selected character and your sound setting locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact aduskun@gmail.com.
